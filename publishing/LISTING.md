## Name

Basic Text-to-Speech

## Summary (the short line under the name, max 250 characters)

Reads articles aloud with a natural voice that runs on your own computer, highlighting
each sentence as it goes. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

## Description

Press the toolbar button on any article and Basic Text-to-Speech reads it aloud.

- **A natural voice that runs on your computer.** It uses Piper, an open-source neural
  voice, inside the add-on itself. The page you are reading is never sent anywhere.
- **Follow along.** Each sentence is highlighted as it is read, and the page scrolls with it.
- **Reads the article, not the clutter.** Menus, ads, sidebars, share buttons and reference
  lists are skipped. Reading stops at a login or paywall box.
- **Simple controls.** A small bar on the page lets you pause, skip, change speed and close.
  Drag it anywhere; it fades out of the way while reading.
- **Right-click to choose.** "Read selection aloud" reads just the text you selected;
  "Read from here" starts at the sentence you clicked.
- **Six English voices** to choose from in Settings.

**About the voice download.** The first time you read, the voice (about 60 MB) is downloaded
once from Hugging Face and kept in Firefox. That is the only thing this add-on downloads, and
nothing is ever uploaded.

**Not supported:** PDFs (Firefox does not allow add-ons inside its PDF viewer) and
Firefox's own pages.

Basic Text-to-Speech is free and open source (GPL-3.0). If you find it useful, you can
support its development with the Contribute button on this page — every feature stays free
either way.
