Credits and licences

Basic Text-to-Speech is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later. Reading happens on this computer; no page text is sent anywhere. The only thing downloaded is the voice you choose, once, from Hugging Face.

Built with

  • Piper — the neural voice engine (MIT)
  • eSpeak NG — turns words into sounds before Piper speaks them (GPL-3.0-or-later)
  • vits-web and piper-wasm by Diffusion Studio — Piper in the browser (MIT; piper-wasm contains eSpeak NG)
  • ONNX Runtime Web by Microsoft (MIT)

Voices

  • Amy — from the Mycroft Mimic 3 voices (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  • HFC Female and HFC Male — trained on the Hi-Fi-CAPTAIN corpus by NICT (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
  • Ryan — trained on RyanSpeech (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)
  • Joe — from the OHF voice datasets (CC0)

All voices were trained by the Piper project.
